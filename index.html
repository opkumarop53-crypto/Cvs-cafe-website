Privacy Policy

What we collect
When you place an order, we ask for your name, phone number, and delivery address. If you tap "Share my current location," we also read your device's GPS coordinates, once, at that moment.

How it's used
These details are submitted directly through our secure order form so our team can prepare and deliver your order and contact you if needed. Nothing you enter is shared with any other business or used for marketing.

Where it's stored
Your order is stored securely, hosted by our website provider (Netlify), and is only visible on our password‑protected delivery dashboard to CVS Cafe staff and delivery partners fulfilling your order. It is not sold, shared publicly, or passed to any third party.

Location access
Location is only requested if you tap the "Share my current location" button, and your browser will always ask your permission first. We do not track your location in the background, and it is only used to help our delivery rider find you for that one order.

Cookies & tracking
This site does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.

Your choices
You can order without sharing your location — just type your address. You're also free to close this page at any time before sending the WhatsApp message; nothing is submitted until you tap "Place order."
