Privacy Policy

What we collect
When you place an order, we ask for your name, phone number, and delivery address. If you tap "Share my current location," we also read your device's GPS coordinates, once, at that moment.

How it's used
These details are submitted to CVS Cafe's order system so our team and delivery partners can prepare and deliver your order and contact you if needed. Nothing you enter is used for marketing or sold.

Where it's stored
Your order is stored securely with our website provider (Netlify) and is only visible to CVS Cafe staff through a password-protected staff page. It is not shared with any other business.

Location access
Location is only requested if you tap the "Share my current location" button, and your browser will always ask your permission first. We do not track your location in the background, and it is only used to help our delivery rider find you for that one order.

Cookies & tracking
This site does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers.

Your choices
You can order without sharing your location — just type your address. You're also free to close this page at any time before placing the order; nothing is submitted until you tap "Place order."

Questions

Cancelling an order
To cancel an order, please call us at +91 82716 51985 as soon as possible. Orders can usually be cancelled free of charge if our kitchen has not yet started preparing them.
